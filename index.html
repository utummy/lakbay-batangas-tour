About Us

Discover the Heart of Batangas

Welcome to Lakbay Batangas Tours! We are your trusted local guide to the beauty and charm of Batangas. From the world-famous Taal Lake and Volcano to our pristine beaches and centuries-old heritage towns, we bring you the very best of our province.

Whether you seek a peaceful escape in nature, an exciting adventure, or a journey through history and culture — we have the perfect tour for you. Every trip is carefully designed to showcase the true spirit of Batangas.

With 30 years of experience and every regional tourism award earned, we are proud to be your reliable partner in travel. Let us show you why Batangas is loved by visitors from near and far!
